# Nara

Only what is specific to Nara; the japan-travel rules still apply. Times are approximate; mark them "约" / "approx." in the draft.

## As a trip
- Usually a day trip from Kyoto or Osaka, not a base. A half day covers Nara Park; a full day adds Naramachi or Horyu-ji.
- Arriving in the morning beats afternoon crowds.

## Areas that go together
- **Nara Park**: Kofuku-ji, Todai-ji (Great Buddha), Kasuga Taisha, the deer. All walkable from Kintetsu-Nara Station, approx. 5–20 min between sights.
- **Naramachi**: old merchant streets, cafes; south of the park, a short walk.
- **Horyu-ji**: one of the oldest wooden temples; separate trip by JR from JR Nara, approx. 15 min plus a walk or bus.

## Getting there
- **From Kyoto**: Kintetsu from Kyoto Station to Kintetsu-Nara, approx. 45 min (limited express faster, reserved seat). JR Nara Line is an option but JR Nara Station is farther from the park.
- **From Osaka**: Kintetsu from Osaka-Namba to Kintetsu-Nara, approx. 40 min.
- Kintetsu-Nara Station is the closest to Nara Park; prefer it.

## Deer and walking
- Deer crackers (shika senbei) are sold in the park; deer can push and nip, especially around food. Keep snacks and paper bags away, take care with small children.
- The park is spread out and hilly towards Kasuga Taisha; comfortable shoes, rests built in.

## Rain and heat
- Rainy-day options: Nara National Museum and Todai-ji's Great Buddha Hall (indoors).
- Summer is hot with little shade on the main paths; plan the park in the morning.

## Seasons
- Omizutori at Todai-ji Nigatsu-do, usually early March: evening fire ritual, crowded.
- Nara Tokae lantern festival, usually August evenings.
- Autumn leaves around Nara Park, usually mid to late November.
