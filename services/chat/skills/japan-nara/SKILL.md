# Nara

Suggestions only, not checked facts. The japan-travel rules decide the plan; the traveller should check anything they rely on.

## Sights
- Nara Park: deer roam freely.
- Todai-ji: the Great Buddha Hall.
- Kasuga Taisha: shrine with hundreds of lanterns.
- Kofuku-ji: five-storey pagoda.
- Naramachi: old merchant streets and cafes.
- Horyu-ji: one of the oldest wooden temples.
- Nara National Museum.

## Deer and walking
- Deer crackers (shika senbei) are sold in the park; deer can push and nip, especially around food. Keep snacks and paper bags away, take care with small children.
- Parts of the park are hilly; comfortable shoes, rests built in.

## Crowds, rain and heat
- Mornings beat afternoon crowds.
- Rainy-day options: Nara National Museum and Todai-ji's Great Buddha Hall (indoors).
- Summer is hot with little shade on the main paths.

## Seasons
- Omizutori at Todai-ji Nigatsu-do, usually early March: evening fire ritual, crowded.
- Nara Tokae lantern festival, usually August evenings.
- Autumn leaves around Nara Park, usually mid to late November.
