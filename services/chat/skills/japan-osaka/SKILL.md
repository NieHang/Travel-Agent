# Osaka

Suggestions only, not checked facts. The japan-travel rules decide the plan; the traveller should check anything they rely on.

## Sights
- Dotonbori: neon-lit canal street of street food.
- Shinsaibashi: covered shopping arcade.
- Kuromon Market: food market.
- Osaka Castle: the castle keep and its park.
- Umeda Sky Building: observation deck.
- Shinsekai and Tsutenkaku: retro district and its tower.
- Abeno Harukas: observation deck.
- Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan.
- Universal Studios Japan: theme park.

## Food
- Takoyaki, okonomiyaki, kushikatsu (Shinsekai is known for it).
- Kuromon Market and Dotonbori for street food.

## Crowds and timing
- Dotonbori is at its best in the evening; Kuromon Market in the morning.
- Observation decks are popular at sunset.

## Universal Studios Japan
- Most visitors spend the whole day; avoid weekends and school holidays if possible.
- Popular areas (e.g. Super Nintendo World) may need timed entry or a paid express pass; put this in Before you go and ask the traveller to check.

## Passes
- Sightseeing passes exist; suggest comparing them with the actual plan, never assume one pays off.

## Day trips
- Kyoto: load the japan-kyoto skill.
- Nara: load the japan-nara skill.
- Kobe: harbour, Kitano, Kobe beef.
- Himeji Castle.

## Seasons
- Cherry blossom: Osaka Castle Park, Expo '70 Park.
- Tenjin Matsuri, usually late July: river procession and fireworks, big crowds.
- Winter illuminations around Midosuji and Umeda.
