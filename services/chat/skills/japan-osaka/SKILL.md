# Osaka

Only what is specific to Osaka; the japan-travel rules still apply. Times are approximate; mark them "约" / "approx." in the draft.

## Areas that go together
- **Minami (Namba / Dotonbori / Shinsaibashi)**: street food, neon, shopping; best in the evening. Kuromon Market in the morning.
- **Kita (Umeda)**: department stores, Umeda Sky Building at sunset, transit hub.
- **Osaka Castle**: park and castle; a morning or half day.
- **Tennoji / Shinsekai**: Tsutenkaku, kushikatsu, Abeno Harukas; pairs with Minami.
- **Bay area**: Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan; Universal Studios Japan is a separate full day.

## Getting in and out
- **Kansai Airport (KIX)**: Nankai rapi:t to Namba, approx. 40 min; JR Haruka to Tennoji / Shin-Osaka, approx. 35–50 min.
- **Itami Airport**: airport bus to Umeda or Namba, approx. 25–30 min.
- **Shinkansen**: Shin-Osaka; Midosuji subway line to Umeda / Namba, approx. 5–15 min.

## Getting around
- An IC card (ICOCA, or any Suica / PASMO) works on Metro, JR and private lines.
- The Midosuji Line links Shin-Osaka, Umeda, Namba and Tennoji; stay along it.
- Sightseeing passes exist; suggest comparing them with the actual plan, never assume one pays off.

## Day trips
- **Kyoto**: approx. 30 min by JR Special Rapid. Load the japan-kyoto skill.
- **Nara**: Kintetsu from Osaka-Namba, approx. 40 min. Load the japan-nara skill.
- **Kobe**: approx. 20–30 min; harbour, Kitano, Kobe beef.
- **Himeji Castle**: approx. 1 h by JR Special Rapid, quicker by Shinkansen.

## Universal Studios Japan
- A full day; avoid weekends and school holidays if possible.
- Popular areas (e.g. Super Nintendo World) may need timed entry or a paid express pass; put this in Before you go and ask the traveller to check.
- From Osaka Station, JR to Universal City, approx. 15 min.

## Seasons
- Cherry blossom: Osaka Castle Park, Expo '70 Park.
- Tenjin Matsuri, usually late July: river procession and fireworks, big crowds.
- Winter illuminations around Midosuji and Umeda.
