# Tokyo

Suggestions only, not checked facts. The japan-travel rules decide the plan; the traveller should check anything they rely on.

## Sights
- Senso-ji: Tokyo's oldest temple.
- Nakamise: traditional snack and souvenir street.
- Tokyo Skytree: observation tower.
- Ueno Park.
- Tokyo National Museum.
- Ameyoko: busy market street.
- Yanaka Ginza: old-style shopping street.
- Akihabara: electronics and anime shops.
- Meiji Jingu: shrine in a forest.
- Takeshita Street: youth fashion street.
- Omotesando: tree-lined avenue of flagship stores.
- Shibuya crossing.
- Shibuya Sky: observation deck.
- Shinjuku Gyoen: large garden.
- Omoide Yokocho and Golden Gai: tiny bars and eateries.
- Tsukiji outer market: food stalls.
- Ginza: department stores and shopping.
- Imperial Palace East Gardens.
- teamLab: digital-art museums.

## Food
- Sushi, ramen, tempura, monjayaki, and depachika (department-store food halls).
- Tsukiji outer market for a food-stall breakfast.

## Crowds and timing
- Meiji Jingu, Senso-ji and the Tsukiji outer market are calmest early in the morning.
- Shibuya crossing is at its busiest at dusk; Omoide Yokocho and Golden Gai are evening places.

## Rainy days
- Tokyo National Museum, teamLab, depachika.

## Reservations
- Timed-entry places usually sell out ahead: teamLab, the Ghibli Museum (advance tickets only), observation decks at sunset (e.g. Shibuya Sky). Put them in Before you go and ask the traveller to check availability.

## Day trips
- Kamakura: the Great Buddha, temples, the coast.
- Nikko: shrines and mountains.
- Hakone: hot springs, views of Mt Fuji in clear weather.
- Mt Fuji / Lake Kawaguchiko: views depend on the weather; keep a backup.
- Yokohama: Chinatown and the waterfront.

## Seasons
- Cherry blossom: Ueno Park, Chidorigafuchi, Meguro River (very crowded).
- Autumn: ginkgo avenue at Meiji Jingu Gaien, usually late November.
- Summer: fireworks such as the Sumida River festival, usually late July; hot and humid.
