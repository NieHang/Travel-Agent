# Tokyo

Only what is specific to Tokyo; the japan-travel rules still apply. Times are approximate; mark them "约" / "approx." in the draft.

## Areas that go together
- **Asakusa + Ueno** (+ Tokyo Skytree): old-town temples, Ameyoko market, Ueno Park and museums. Good arrival-day area if staying in Ueno.
- **Shibuya + Harajuku + Omotesando**: Meiji Jingu in the morning, Takeshita Street and Omotesando, Shibuya crossing at dusk.
- **Shinjuku**: Shinjuku Gyoen (garden), Omoide Yokocho and Golden Gai in the evening; the station is huge, allow extra time to find exits.
- **Ginza + Tokyo Station / Marunouchi + Tsukiji outer market**: shopping, morning food stalls, Imperial Palace East Gardens.
- **Odaiba / Toyosu**: waterfront and digital-art museums; a half day.
- **Akihabara**: electronics and anime; pairs with Ueno.

## Getting in and out
- **Narita**: Keisei Skyliner to Ueno, approx. 45 min; Narita Express to Tokyo / Shinjuku, approx. 60–90 min. Plenty of time is needed on departure day.
- **Haneda**: Keikyu Line to Shinagawa, approx. 15–20 min; Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho, approx. 20 min. Closer to the city than Narita.
- **To Kyoto / Osaka**: Tokaido Shinkansen from Tokyo or Shinagawa; Nozomi to Kyoto approx. 2 h 15 min, to Shin-Osaka approx. 2 h 30 min.

## Getting around
- An IC card (Suica / PASMO) covers JR, Tokyo Metro, Toei subway and buses.
- The JR Yamanote Line loops through Tokyo, Shinjuku, Shibuya, Ueno and Akihabara; Ginza Line links Shibuya, Ginza, Ueno and Asakusa.
- Rush hour is heavy; on hotel-change days avoid crowded lines with large luggage.

## Day trips
- **Hakone**: Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku, approx. 85 min; hot springs and views of Mt Fuji (weather permitting). Better as an overnight ryokan stay than a rushed day.
- **Kamakura**: JR from Tokyo, approx. 1 h; Great Buddha, temples, coast.
- **Nikko**: approx. 2 h by Tobu or Shinkansen + local line; a full day.
- **Mt Fuji / Lake Kawaguchiko**: express bus from Shinjuku, approx. 2 h; views depend on the weather, keep a backup.
- **Yokohama**: approx. 30 min; an easy half day.

## Reservations and crowds
- Timed-entry places usually sell out ahead: digital-art museums (teamLab), the Ghibli Museum (advance tickets only), observation decks at sunset (e.g. Shibuya Sky). Put them in Before you go and ask the traveller to check availability.
- Meiji Jingu, Senso-ji and the Tsukiji outer market are calmest early in the morning.

## Seasons
- Cherry blossom: Ueno Park, Chidorigafuchi, Meguro River (very crowded).
- Autumn: ginkgo avenue at Meiji Jingu Gaien, usually late November.
- Summer: fireworks such as the Sumida River festival, usually late July; hot and humid, plan indoor afternoons.
