## Practical
- Site normally closed on Mondays, moving to the next weekday after a public holiday Monday.[^closure1]
- Site closed: 2026-12-15, 2026-12-26, 2026-12-27, 2026-12-28, 2026-12-29, 2026-12-30, 2026-12-31.[^closure2]
- Main galleries (Honkan, Heiseikan, Toyokan) closed from 2026-12-22 through 2026-12-25.[^closure3]
- Rail access: JR, subway, private rail.[^access]
