Allow about 20 minutes to cross the bridge and take in the river and the wooded hills behind it. It is free and open at any hour, and makes the natural start or end of a day in [[kyoto/arashiyama]].

## Practical
- The bridge is busiest in spring and autumn.[^busy]
- Rail access: Randen Arashiyama Station, 2 minutes on foot.[^randen]
- Rail access: Hankyu Arashiyama Station, 8 minutes on foot.[^hankyu]
