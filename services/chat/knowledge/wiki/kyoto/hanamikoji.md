Allow about 45 minutes for the lantern-lit teahouse street through the south side of Gion, ending at [[kyoto/kennin-ji]]. It is a public street, free at any hour, and best at dusk. People live and work here: keep moving, stay out of the side alleys and leave geiko and maiko alone. See [[kyoto/higashiyama]].

## Practical
- Do not block the road by waiting on the pavement, stopping for photos or stepping into the roadway.[^blocking]
- The street is not pedestrian-only; cars use it.[^cars]
- Do not stop, touch, follow or photograph geiko and maiko without permission.[^geiko]
