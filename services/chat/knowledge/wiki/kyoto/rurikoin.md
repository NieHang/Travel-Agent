Allow about an hour. The draw is the maple garden reflected in the polished floor and desk of the upper room. The temple opens only for seasonal special viewings, so confirm the dates before building a day around it; see [[kyoto/reservations]] and [[kyoto/north]].

## Practical
- Open only for special viewings in spring (Apr–May), summer (Jul–Aug) and autumn (Oct–Dec).[^seasons]
- Autumn 2026 viewing: Oct 1–Dec 13.[^autumn]
- Visits from Nov 13 to Dec 6, 2026 need advance booking on the temple website.[^booking]
- Rail access: Eizan railway Yase-Hieizanguchi Station, 13 minutes on foot; change from Keihan at Demachiyanagi.[^access]
