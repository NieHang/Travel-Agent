Allow about 90 minutes.

## Practical
- Closed Mondays; open on a holiday Monday and closed the next Tuesday instead.[^closure]
- Closed 2026-12-28 through 2027-01-01.[^new-year]
- Special exhibition on The Tale of Genji until 2026-11-29: 09:00–17:30, Fridays to 20:00, last entry 30 minutes before closing; adults 2,200 yen.[^special]
- Collection galleries 2026-12-22 to 2026-12-27 and 2027-01-02 to 2027-03-22: 09:30–17:00, Fridays to 20:00; adults 700 yen.[^collection]
- Garden only, galleries closed: 2026-12-01 to 2026-12-20 and 2027-03-24 to 2027-04-18; 09:30–17:00, adults 300 yen.[^garden]
- Rail access: Keihan to Shichijo Station, 7 minutes on foot.[^access]
