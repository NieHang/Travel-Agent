Allow about 60 minutes for the pond garden; the halls and the dragon ceiling painting are separate add-ons. The north gate leads toward the bamboo grove; see [[kyoto/arashiyama]].

## Practical
- Garden 08:30–17:00, last admission 16:50.[^hours]
- Special early opening of the garden from 07:30, 2026-11-14 through 2026-11-30 (garden entrance only).[^early]
- Halls 08:30–16:45, last admission 16:30; closed on some event days.[^halls]
- Dragon painting in the Hatto 09:00–16:30, shown on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays only, daily for periods in spring and autumn.[^dragon]
- Dragon painting closed 2027-01-02.[^dragon-closed]
- Rail access: private rail, JR.[^access]
