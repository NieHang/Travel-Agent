Allow about 90 minutes.

## Practical
- 2026 hours: Jan 1–Mar 26 06:00–18:00, Mar 27–Apr 5 06:00–21:30, Apr 6–Jun 30 06:00–18:00, Jul 1–Aug 13 06:00–18:30, Aug 14–16 06:00–21:30, Aug 17–31 06:00–18:30, Sep 1–Nov 20 06:00–18:00, Nov 21–30 06:00–21:30, Dec 06:00–18:00; last entry 21:00 on night-viewing days.[^hours]
- The night-viewing periods change every year.[^night]
- The grounds can be entered only by climbing Kiyomizu-zaka or Chawan-zaka; map apps may suggest routes that do not lead in.[^entry]
- From Kyoto Station: city bus 206 or 100 to Gojozaka, then 10 minutes on foot.[^bus]
- Rail access: Keihan Kiyomizu-Gojo Station, about 25 minutes on foot.[^access]
