The northern valleys are cooler, greener and slower than the city, and each one takes at least half a day because of the travel. Choose one valley per day.

For a shrine and forest walk, pair [[kyoto/kifune-shrine]] with [[kyoto/kurama-dera]]; in summer the Kibune restaurants serve meals on platforms over the river. For moss gardens and a farm village, take the bus to Ohara for [[kyoto/sanzen-in]]. For maples reflected in a polished floor, check whether [[kyoto/rurikoin]] is open. Closer to the centre, [[kyoto/shimogamo-shrine]] sits in an old forest by the river fork.
