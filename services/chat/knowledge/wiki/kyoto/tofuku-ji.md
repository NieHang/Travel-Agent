Allow about 60 minutes.

## Practical
- Viewing hours: Apr 1–Nov 13 09:00–16:00; Nov 14–Dec 6 Tsutenkyo Bridge from 08:30 and the Hojo garden from 09:00, both to 16:00; Dec 7–Mar 31 09:00–15:30. Admission ends at closing and the gates shut 30 minutes later.[^hours]
- The autumn viewing dates change every year.[^autumn]
- Tsutenkyo Bridge and Kaisando: adults 600 yen, 1,000 yen Nov 14–Dec 6, when no combined ticket is sold.[^price]
- Hojo garden: adults 600 yen from 2026-11-01.[^hojo]
- Rail access: JR Nara Line or Keihan to Tofukuji Station, about 10 minutes on foot.[^access]
