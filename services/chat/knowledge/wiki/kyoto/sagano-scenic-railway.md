A slow open-air train through the Hozu River gorge, best in autumn and spring. Pair it with [[kyoto/arashiyama]]; seats sell out in peak seasons, see [[kyoto/reservations]].

## Practical
- One way covers about 7 km in about 25 minutes.[^route]
- Advance tickets go on sale online at midnight one month before the ride date.[^advance]
- All seats are reserved; once they sell out, standing tickets are sold on the day at the stations except Torokko Hozukyo.[^sameday]
- Free cancellation until 23:59 the day before; no refunds on the day.[^cancel]
- The Japan Rail Pass is not accepted.[^jrpass]
- 2026 calendar: no trains Jan 1–Feb 28 and Dec 30–31 (winter break); other no-service days are marked on the calendar.[^winter]
