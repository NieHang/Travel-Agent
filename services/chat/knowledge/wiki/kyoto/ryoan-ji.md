Allow about 45 minutes for the rock garden of fifteen stones in raked gravel, the pond and the grounds. Come early, before the viewing veranda fills. It is close to [[kyoto/kinkaku-ji]]; see [[kyoto/northwest]].

## Practical
- Open daily 08:00–17:00; Dec–Feb admission starts 08:30 and closing is 16:30.[^hours]
- Open every day of the year.[^closure]
- Access by Keifuku (Randen) train to Ryoanji Station, 8 minutes on foot, or by city or JR bus to the Ryoanji-mae stop.[^access]
