Allow about 60 minutes for the Sanmon gate and the Hojo garden. See [[kyoto/higashiyama]] for nearby walks.

## Practical
- Viewing hours: Dec 1–Feb 28 08:40–16:30, Mar 1–Nov 30 08:40–17:00.[^hours]
- Admission ends 20 minutes before closing.[^last]
- November 2026: viewing 08:40–17:00, extended to 17:15 on weekends and holidays.[^november]
- Closed to general viewing Dec 28–31; open over New Year.[^closure]
- Nanzen-in is closed for roof works until spring 2027; the grounds, Hojo garden and Sanmon stay open.[^nanzenin]
- Rail access: subway (Tozai Line, Keage Station, 10 minutes on foot).[^access]
