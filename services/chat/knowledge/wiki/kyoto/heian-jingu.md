Allow about 45 minutes for the giant torii, the wide vermilion courtyard and the main hall; add time for the paid garden behind it. It sits in the Okazaki museum district, a short walk from [[kyoto/nanzen-ji]]; see [[kyoto/higashiyama]].

## Practical
- Mar 1–Sep 30: 06:00–18:00.[^hours]
- October: 06:00–17:30.[^october]
- Nov 1–end of February: 06:00–17:00.[^winter]
- Rail access: Higashiyama Station on the Tozai subway line, 10 minutes on foot from exit 1.[^access]
- From Kyoto Station: city bus 5 or 100 to Okazaki Koen Bijutsukan/Heian Jingu-mae, then 5 minutes on foot.[^bus]
