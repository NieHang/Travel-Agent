Allow about 30 minutes. The path is a public lane, free and open at any hour, and it is far calmer soon after sunrise; see [[kyoto/crowds]]. Enter from the north gate of [[kyoto/tenryu-ji]] to join it without doubling back; see [[kyoto/arashiyama]].

## Practical
- The path runs about 400 metres from Nonomiya Shrine past the north gate of Tenryu-ji to Okochi Sanso.[^path]
- Rail access: Randen Arashiyama Station, 10 minutes on foot.[^randen]
- Rail access: JR Saga-Arashiyama Station, 13 minutes on foot.[^jr]
