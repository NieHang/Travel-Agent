Allow about 90 minutes for the Ninomaru Palace and gardens. See [[kyoto/central]] for what to combine it with.

## Practical
- Castle entry 08:45–16:00, closing at 17:00.[^hours]
- Ninomaru Palace admission 08:45–16:10.[^ninomaru]
- Honmaru Palace admission 09:30–16:00, by advance timed web ticket only.[^honmaru]
- Castle closed Dec 29–31.[^closure]
- Ninomaru Palace closed every Tuesday in January, July, August and December (the next day when that Tuesday is a holiday), Dec 26–28 and Jan 1–3.[^ninomaru-closed]
- Honmaru Palace closed on the third Monday of each month and the following day (open when that day is a holiday), Dec 26–28 and Jan 1–3.[^honmaru-closed]
- Rail access from Kyoto Station: subway or JR.[^access]
