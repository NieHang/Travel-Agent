Allow about 60 minutes for the Golden Pavilion and its pond garden. See [[kyoto/northwest]] for what to combine it with.

## Practical
- Open daily 09:00–17:00, with no closed days.[^hours]
- Access is by Kyoto City Bus to the Kinkakuji-michi stop; the official access page lists no rail station.[^access]
