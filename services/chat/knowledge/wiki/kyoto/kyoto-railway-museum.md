Allow about 2 hours for the train halls, the roundhouse and the simulators. Mostly indoors, so it suits families and [[kyoto/rain]]. It is a short trip west of Kyoto Station; see [[kyoto/kyoto-station]].

## Practical
- Open 10:00–17:00, last entry 16:30.[^hours]
- Closed Wednesdays (open on public holidays and in spring and summer school breaks) and Dec 30–Jan 1; some other days are also closed, so check the museum's calendar.[^closure]
- Adults 1,500 yen, rising to 1,900 yen from 2027-03-01; the steam train ride and driving simulator cost extra.[^price]
- Former Nijo Station building closed for works from 2026-10-01 for about 15 months.[^works]
- Rail access: JR Sagano Line to Umekoji-Kyotonishi Station, 2 minutes on foot.[^access]
