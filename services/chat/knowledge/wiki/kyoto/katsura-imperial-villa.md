Allow about 60 minutes for the guided garden route. Visits need an application, so check [[kyoto/reservations]] before fixing the day.

## Practical
- Visits are by application to the Imperial Household Agency, online in advance or on the day.[^apply]
- No visits on Mondays (the next day when Monday is a holiday), Dec 28–Jan 4, or days with official events.[^closure]
- Rail access: private rail (Hankyu Kyoto Line, Katsura Station, 20 minutes on foot).[^access]
