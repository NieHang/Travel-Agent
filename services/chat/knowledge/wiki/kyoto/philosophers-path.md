Allow about 45 minutes to walk the canal path one way, more with café stops. It is free and open at any hour, and best in cherry blossom season and autumn. Start at [[kyoto/ginkaku-ji]] and walk south toward [[kyoto/nanzen-ji]]; see [[kyoto/higashiyama]].

## Practical
- The path runs about 2 km between Ginkaku-ji and Nyakuoji Shrine, near Nanzen-ji.[^path]
- Bus access to the north end: Ginkakuji-michi stop, by city bus 102 or 203 from Imadegawa subway station.[^bus]
