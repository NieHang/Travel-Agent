Allow about an hour for the twin dragons on the hall ceiling, the Wind and Thunder Gods screen and the quiet gardens. It stands at the south end of [[kyoto/hanamikoji]] and is mostly indoors, so it also works in rain; see [[kyoto/higashiyama]].

## Practical
- Hours: 10:00 until last entry at 16:30; gates close at 17:00.[^hours]
- Admission: adults 800 yen; school students 500 yen; free below school age.[^price]
- Rail access: Keihan Gion-Shijo Station, 7 minutes on foot.[^access]
- Bus access: Higashiyama Yasui stop, 5 minutes on foot.[^bus]
