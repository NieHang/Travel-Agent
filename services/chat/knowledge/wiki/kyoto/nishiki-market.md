Allow about 90 minutes.

## Practical
- No market-wide closing day; hours vary by shop, and many shops close on Wednesdays.[^closure]
- Many shops are closed Jan 1–3.[^new-year]
- Eating while walking is not allowed; eat at or inside the shop.[^etiquette]
- Rail access: Shijo subway station or Hankyu Karasuma Station, about 3 minutes on foot.[^access]
