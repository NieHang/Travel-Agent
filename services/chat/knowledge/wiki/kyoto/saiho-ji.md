The moss garden is open only to visitors who applied in advance, and the visit starts with sutra copying in the main hall. Allow about 60 minutes. Fix the date early; see [[kyoto/reservations]]. It sits in the far west of the city, so pair it with [[kyoto/katsura-imperial-villa]] or [[kyoto/arashiyama]].

## Practical
- Visits need an application made by the day before.[^apply]
- Apply online (account required) or by return postcard.[^how]
- Groups of five or more are not accepted.[^group]
- Regular visits are suspended 2027-01-12 through 2027-02-26 for grounds maintenance.[^winter]
- The garden paths cannot be used with a wheelchair.[^wheelchair]
- Access by Kyoto Bus route 73 to the Koke-dera Suzumushi-dera stop, then 3 minutes on foot.[^access]
