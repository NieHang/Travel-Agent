Allow about 90 minutes to climb the lantern-lined steps and walk on to the middle and inner shrines. The valley is reached by the Eizan railway and then a bus or a riverside walk. Pair it with [[kyoto/kurama-dera]] over the hill; see [[kyoto/north]].

## Practical
- Main shrine: 06:00–20:00 from May 1 to Nov 30.[^hours]
- Open all year; entry is free.[^free]
- The amulet and goshuin counter stays open longer during illumination periods.[^office]
- Parking is very limited; the shrine recommends public transport.[^parking]
