Allow about an hour for the five-storey pagoda, Japan's tallest wooden tower, and the Buddhist statues of the lecture hall. It is a walk from the south side of the station, so it fits an arrival or departure day; see [[kyoto/kyoto-station]].

## Practical
- Gates open 05:00–17:00.[^gates]
- Kondo and Kodo halls: 08:00–17:00, last entry 16:30.[^halls]
- The grounds, Miedo and Jikido are free; the Kondo and Kodo halls are paid.[^free]
- The pagoda interior and the treasure hall open only during special viewing periods, and prices change with them.[^special]
- Rail access: Kintetsu Toji Station, 10 minutes on foot.[^access]
- From Kyoto Station: 15 minutes on foot from the Hachijo exit.[^walk]
