Allow about an hour for the long hall and its rows of Kannon statues. It is indoors, so it works on a wet day, and it sits beside [[kyoto/kyoto-national-museum]]; see [[kyoto/higashiyama]].

## Practical
- Apr 1–Nov 15: 08:30–17:00.[^hours]
- Nov 16–Mar 31: 09:00–16:00.[^winter]
- Last entry is 30 minutes before closing.[^last]
- Admission: adults 600 yen.[^price]
- From Kyoto Station: city bus 100, 206 or 208, about 10 minutes.[^bus]
- Rail access: Keihan Shichijo Station, about 7 minutes on foot.[^access]
