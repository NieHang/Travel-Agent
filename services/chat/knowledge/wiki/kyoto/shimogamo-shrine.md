Allow about an hour, including the walk through the old Tadasu no Mori forest to the vermilion gate. It stands where the Kamo and Takano rivers meet, so it pairs with a riverside walk; see [[kyoto/north]].

## Practical
- Inner precinct: gate opens 06:30 and closes 17:00.[^hours]
- From Kyoto Station: city bus 4 or 205 to Shimogamo Jinja-mae or Tadasunomori-mae.[^bus]
- Rail access: Keihan Demachiyanagi Station, 12 minutes on foot.[^access]
