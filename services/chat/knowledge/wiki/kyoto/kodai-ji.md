Allow about an hour for the gardens, teahouses and small bamboo grove. It lies between [[kyoto/yasaka-shrine]] and [[kyoto/sannenzaka-ninenzaka]], and its seasonal night illuminations make it an evening option; see [[kyoto/higashiyama]].

## Practical
- Hours: 09:00–17:30, last entry 17:00.[^hours]
- During night special viewings the grounds are lit from 17:00 to 22:00, last entry 21:30.[^night]
- Admission: adults 800 yen.[^price]
- The bamboo grove is inside the garden and needs the temple admission.[^bamboo]
