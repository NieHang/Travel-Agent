Allow about 90 minutes for the moss garden and halls, plus the walk up the approach from the Ohara bus stop. The village is a long bus ride from the centre, so give it half a day; see [[kyoto/north]].

## Practical
- March to October: 09:00–17:00.[^hours]
- November: 08:45–16:45.[^november]
- December to February: 09:00–16:30.[^winter]
- Admission: adults 700 yen.[^price]
- When roads are busy, the subway plus bus route keeps steadier time than the direct bus from Kyoto Station.[^route]
- There is no temple car park; use private car parks near the Ohara bus stop.[^parking]
