Allow about two hours for the climb through cedar forest to the main hall and its valley view. Fit walkers can continue over the hill on an unpaved trail down to [[kyoto/kifune-shrine]]; wear proper shoes and skip the trail in bad weather. See [[kyoto/north]].

## Practical
- Mountain entry fee: 500 yen.[^fee]
- A cable car covers the lower slope: adults 200 yen one way.[^cable]
- On the trail over the hill, Mao-den to Kifune Shrine takes 20 minutes on foot.[^trail]
- Rail access: Eizan railway from Demachiyanagi to Kurama Station, 30 minutes.[^access]
