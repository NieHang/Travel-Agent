Allow about 60 minutes for the Silver Pavilion and its garden. It suits a walk that continues along the Philosopher’s Path; see [[kyoto/higashiyama]].

## Practical
- Open daily with no closed days: Mar 1–Nov 30 08:30–17:00, Dec 1–end of Feb 09:00–16:30.[^hours]
- Access is by Kyoto City Bus to the Ginkakuji-michi stop; the official access page lists no rail station.[^access]
- Parts of the grounds have stairs and cannot be reached with a wheelchair or stroller; a detour route is signed.[^stairs]
