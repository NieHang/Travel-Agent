Start with [[kyoto/kiyomizu-dera]] for its wooden stage and city views. Choose Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka for a walk past small shops on the stone lanes. Consider Yasaka Shrine or the townhouses and teahouses of Gion. For an indoor stop, see [[kyoto/kyoto-national-museum]].

For a separate walk, consider Ginkaku-ji (the Silver Pavilion), the canal along the Philosopher’s Path, or Nanzen-ji.
