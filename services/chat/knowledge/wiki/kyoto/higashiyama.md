Start with [[kyoto/kiyomizu-dera]] for its wooden stage and city views. Walk down [[kyoto/sannenzaka-ninenzaka]] past small shops on the stone lanes, with [[kyoto/kodai-ji]] as a garden stop. Continue to [[kyoto/yasaka-shrine]], then the teahouses of Gion along [[kyoto/hanamikoji]] to [[kyoto/kennin-ji]]. For an indoor stop, see [[kyoto/sanjusangen-do]] or [[kyoto/kyoto-national-museum]].

For a separate walk, start at [[kyoto/ginkaku-ji]] (the Silver Pavilion), follow the canal along the [[kyoto/philosophers-path]] to [[kyoto/nanzen-ji]], and finish at [[kyoto/heian-jingu]].
