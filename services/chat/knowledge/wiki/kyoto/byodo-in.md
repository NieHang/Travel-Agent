The Phoenix Hall stands in Uji, south of Kyoto, a short walk from Uji's train stations. Allow about 40 minutes for the exterior and the Hoshokan museum, or up to two hours with the interior tour and tea on the grounds. In busy seasons, buy the interior-tour ticket first. Pair it with matcha shops along the Uji River.

## Practical
- Garden 08:45–17:30, last entry 17:15.[^garden]
- Phoenix Hall interior tours 09:30–16:10, 35 people each, tickets from 09:10 while they last.[^interior]
- Hoshokan museum 09:00–17:00, last entry 16:45.[^museum]
- Garden and museum open every day; interior tours may stop for bad weather or ceremonies.[^closure]
- One ticket covers the garden and museum, adults 700 yen; there is no garden-only ticket.[^price]
- Interior tour: a further 300 yen per person.[^interior-price]
- The interior-tour desk takes cash only.[^cash]
