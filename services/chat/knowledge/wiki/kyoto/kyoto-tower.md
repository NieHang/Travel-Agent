Allow about 45 minutes for the observation deck. It stays open into the evening, so it suits an arrival day or a night view; see [[kyoto/kyoto-station]].

## Practical
- Observation deck: 10:00–21:00.[^hours]
- Last entry is 20:30.[^last]
- Open every day of the year.[^closure]
- Hours can change without notice.[^change]
- Rail access: 2 minutes on foot from the central exit of JR Kyoto Station.[^access]
